At **Pinc Technology**, we believe in empowering the next generation of engineers, data scientists, and developers. **Pinc - University** is our dedicated open-source hub and collaborative workspace for partnering with academic institutions. Here, we share industry-standard practices, co-develop innovative solutions, and explore the massive potential of maritime data.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

We actively collaborate with multiple universities to bring real-world industrial context into the classroom. Our core focus areas include:

*   🚢 **AIS Telemetry & Maritime Analytics:** Processing massive vessel movement datasets and building robust tracking APIs using modern data engineering tools like DuckDB and Parquet.
*   🌍 **CO2 Emissions & Sustainability:** Leveraging data to monitor, analyze, and reduce the carbon footprint within the maritime and logistics sectors.
*   🛰️ **Satellite Data Integration:** Fusing satellite imagery and telemetry with ground-level data to create comprehensive geospatial intelligence.
*   ⚙️ **Modern DevOps & Infrastructure:** Sharing best practices in automated deployments, CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) to prepare students for enterprise-level cloud environments.

## 🤝 Industrial Sharing & Partnerships

We regularly host **Industrial Sharing Sessions** with university faculties and student bodies. These sessions dive deep into:
- The realities of managing high-availability infrastructure across regional data centers.
- The transition from raw satellite and AIS data to actionable web dashboards.
- How modern tech stacks are transforming traditional maritime operations.
